6. Commands

You can use these commands both in the SysConsol command line (csCommandLine) and in scripts, provided that the specific instruction is permitted for the current context—that is, whether it is allowed during simulation and in the active operating mode (omInteractive or omScript). In terms of syntax, comments must begin with a semicolon (‘;’), and character strings must be enclosed in quotation marks (“). Values (such as index or address) may be specified either directly or by referencing register contents (e.g., R0 * –*RF); however, registers themselves must be specified solely by their names (R0–RF).
