T4001ROM from TMemory class in memory_4001rom unit

T4001ROM is the Intel 4001 ROM and I/O chip plugin of the CoreLAB framework. It is derived from TMemory and implements the IMCS4Bus interface. The component provides a 256-byte ROM area and access to a 4-bit I/O port.
